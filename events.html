FAQ

Questions people usually have

A few quick answers before you RSVP or join a meetup.

Is it free to attend?

Yes. For now, the meetups are free to attend, but RSVP is required so we can manage capacity and keep the experience smooth for everyone.

What language are the events in?

Usually a mix of English and Spanish, depending on who is in the room. The goal is to keep it natural, welcoming, and easy to participate in.

Do I need to be technical to join?

No. The community is for people building tech products, so that includes developers, technical founders, product-minded operators, and non-technical founders actively building.

How often do you host events?

The goal is to host events monthly, with activity growing across Riviera Maya over time as the community expands.

Can I present something I'm building?

Most likely, yes. Some sessions are more presentation-friendly than others, but sharing what you're building, asking for feedback, or proposing a topic is very welcome.
